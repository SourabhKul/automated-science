## Interpretation and limits

This is evidence that the local scaffold is runnable under small explicit budgets. The rejected proposals show that generation, validation, simulation, fitting, scoring, and artifact recording all executed on real code paths. The seed's lower score does not establish that the Lotka–Volterra form is scientifically correct, and the Qwen candidates' worse adaptive validation score does not independently falsify their mechanisms.

The suffix score is adaptive validation because the outer loop ranks proposals on it and sends diagnostics into later prompts. It must not be described as an untouched generalization test. The run also exercises the known inference limitations without correcting them: the implementation selects top-k particles, clips later proposals at prior boundaries, and evaluates the transition with a Gaussian density; weights and posterior summaries were not independently calibrated here. The eight-particle, two-generation setting is an integration budget, not a reliable posterior estimate. The data are deterministic synthetic data from the configured seed model, with no external experiment or independent replicate.

The next falsifiable steps are to run the finite-epsilon analytic and parameter-recovery checks in `reports/inference-repair-acceptance-plan-2026-09-13.md`, then establish a three-way train/validation/sealed-test protocol before scaling. A subsequent discovery run should retain competing mechanisms and report independent trajectories or intervention conditions; it should not promote this smoke's adaptive suffix score as generalization evidence. The five-hour heartbeat should first check that no stale jobs exist, then use this artifact as the starting checkpoint.

The two algebraically equivalent proposals also identify a concrete efficiency follow-up: add a semantic or canonical-form deduplication diagnostic before spending another full inference budget on a redundant candidate. That is an engineering ablation target, not evidence that general symbolic equivalence is solved; any deduplication rule should preserve genuinely distinct mechanisms.
