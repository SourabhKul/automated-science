## Interpretation, failures, and next step

The Qwen choice is a plausible development hypothesis given the earlier training-only cubic residual signal; it is not a discovered physical law. The limiting event was reference-kernel transition efficiency at bounded support, not compute, memory, or model-server availability. The observed second-population acceptance counts are training-only diagnostics. They justify designing a **new, explicitly versioned and independently reviewed** attempt-budget or proposal-kernel protocol; they do not justify changing this run's thresholds, treating partial populations as complete, or peeking at validation/test outcomes. Compare any BDSS or support-aware transition against the mathematically consistent reference with calibration and matched cost-quality evidence. Preserve this unresolved run and its negative result. No second fit or sealed evaluation occurred in this wake. The `validation_accessed:false` receipts should be read narrowly: no selection/evaluation API used validation, but the development loader had already converted the validation target values and retained them in memory. That loader was subsequently repaired to defer target conversion until post-fit verification; the final independent read-only review approved the repair after 45 synthetic tests. The real pilot predates that repair and is not retroactively recharacterized.
