## Verified training-only diagnostic

| Family | Minimum RMSE (V) | Median RMSE (V) | Draws ≤2 V | Draws ≤3 V | Draws ≤5 V |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| S0 | 1.5015 | 5.3968 | 3/512 | 62/512 | 228/512 |
| O2 | 1.4560 | 4.4256 | 8/512 | 114/512 | 448/512 |
| C2 | 1.4549 | 4.4700 | 8/512 | 113/512 | 448/512 |

No draw reached 1 V RMSE. Across the same 512 paired draws, the median
`O2−C2` RMSE difference was exactly zero; its 5th–95th percentile range
was −0.1063 to +0.0168 V. This **does not** identify overflow or sensor
clipping: the two surrogates can make identical predictions under many
draws, and one correlated training record cannot determine the physical
cause. Of 512 complete traces, 124 O2 and 142 C2 traces spent at least 90%
of outputs exactly at the imposed 10 V ceiling. This is a warning about the
coarse surrogate/prior, not evidence that the real tank has that occupancy.
The low fraction at ≤2 V suggests a strict future ABC tolerance may exhaust
attempt caps under this box; it is not an estimate of posterior mass or a
proof that better-fitting parameter settings do not exist. The screen is
**not** an ABC posterior, model-selection comparison, independent validation,
or scientific discovery.
