real tank Qwen call, ABC fit, development-target score, or official test read
occurred. This bounded, noiseless **synthetic-only** exercise tested whether
the checked ABC-SMC caller-array bridge can preserve ambiguity when O2 and C2

## Verified outcome and limits

An independent Luna reviewer replayed all six fits from the saved settings
with the hashed code. Target hashes, statuses, accepted counts, attempts,
ESS, and complete-fit H summaries matched. The result is **overall
incomplete** because both crossing wrong-family first populations exhausted
their cap with zero accepts. No H posterior was computed or promoted for
either incomplete fit.

| O2 H=3 → O2 | Complete | 153, 101 | 31.984 | 3.0136 |
| O2 H=3 → C2 | Incomplete, 0 accepts | 1,024 | — | — |
| C2 H=3 → O2 | Incomplete, 0 accepts | 1,024 | — | — |
| C2 H=3 → C2 | Complete | 146, 137 | 31.964 | 3.0008 |

| O2 H=90 → C2 | Complete | 32, 48 | 30.292 | 93.5 (descriptive only) |

The H=90 raw lower-state maximum was 82.9124, below threshold; O2 and C2
observations were exactly identical. Both fits had zero RMSE with broad H
support, so no mechanism decision is possible. In the H=3 crossing cases,
same-family terminal populations completed near H=3; wrong-family zero
acceptance under the preset tolerance and cap is **inconclusive**, not a
Bayes factor or definitive family rejection. All other parameters were
fixed at truth, so neither outcome shows that full O2/C2 models can be
discriminated from a noisy real record. The two incomplete fits must not be
silently discarded from an overall success count.

The worker elapsed 0.789 seconds, with reported peak RSS 40,878,080 bytes;
the independent replay took 0.55 seconds and about 38 MB peak RSS. A single
worker was used. macOS rejected process address-space/data/RSS limits, so an
external watchdog sampled RSS every 0.25 seconds and would cancel at 1 GiB,
with possible brief between-sample overshoot; a 300-second wall timeout was
also present. Three earlier launcher attempts failed **before generation or
fitting** (resource-limit ordering, unsupported data-segment limit, then a
one-directory-high import root). Their receipts are preserved in
`launch_failures.json`. An independent read-only launcher audit located the
exact import-path defect and confirmed unchanged scientific settings before
one targeted successful launch. The reviewed `result.json` has six fit
records and retains the earlier failures.

## Next gate

The control supports keeping an explicit structural-abstention rule. It
does not justify applying H≈3, its tolerances, or its one-parameter recovery
to the real tank. Before a real Qwen or ABC pilot, establish synthetic
multi-parameter recovery and coverage with nuisance initial states and
model mismatch; use training-only evidence to freeze numeric priors,

forecast gate before any deferred development-target reader. Freeze the
exact Qwen request, matched baseline and selection rule, full-estimation

resource limits, new run ID, and single-use final rule. Have independent
