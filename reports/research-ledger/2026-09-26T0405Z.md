The next diagnostic proposal is receipt-only first: log the first failed step,
maximum finite prefix magnitude, and training-window identity under the
existing divergence rule, and record local AR(2) stability margins for prior
draws and accepted particles over a **training-derived** output envelope.
For a linear recurrence, the Schur-stable region is
`-1 < a2 < 1` and `a2 - 1 < a1 < 1 - a2`. The cubic model's local derivative
requires its effective `a1` across that envelope. Two Luna reviewers
independently computed the result from the terminal development fit receipt:
57/64 nonlinear particles meet those local conditions for every output in
the symmetric training envelope `[-0.17284, +0.17284]`. That bound is the
receipt's `training_max_abs_y`; its training-only scales are
`s_y=0.0605984528271096` and `s_feature=0.0007099938530121302` in
`a1_effective = a1 + 3*d*(s_y/s_feature)*y^2`. This is a receipt-derived
local diagnostic, not a global stability or forecast guarantee, and no
stability-diagnostic receipt was generated by the original v2 run.
An early rejection at the unchanged divergence threshold should preserve the
accepted set if it does not clip or reclassify a path. Imposing stability as a
hard prior condition, narrowing the cubic coefficient, changing the feature,
or changing the divergence threshold **changes the inference target** and
requires a new frozen protocol and calibration. Proposal-kernel changes also
require correct mixture-density weights. No such change or new run occurred
in this review. The reviewer authored the original fit module, so this
methods assessment is coupled and needs independent review before adoption.
