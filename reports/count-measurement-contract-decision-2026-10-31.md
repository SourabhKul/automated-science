## Estimand and eligibility contract

The proposed estimand is the decay-rate time shape conditional on an observed
total count, a declared grouping and a known relative exposure profile.
Conditioning can remove a common unknown amplitude under the specified
independent Poisson-bin model. It does not predict absolute future count
rates, learn that amplitude, or generally integrate it under a Bayesian prior.
Zero total count gives no shape information. No new derivation or computation
is needed to make those limits explicit.

## Acceptance, rejection and disposition

A source could meet this contract only if its own documented raw-bin,
relative-exposure, background, event-loss and grouping facts support the
chosen conditional model before code/data selection. Nuisance uncertainty
must either cancel under a justified factorization or remain in an explicitly
different model. It must not be silently fixed to make a scalar reference.
No physical-replication requirement is added to a within-source estimand.
Claims across independent sources would need a separate experimental design.

Reject readiness when the above support is absent, when the proposed values
are merely corrected peak areas, or when unexplained backgrounds/losses make
the one-amplitude factorization unjustified. Reject a claim of absolute-rate
prediction based only on conditional shape. A zero-total record is uninformative,
not a failed particle to replace. This contract does not alter the previous
yeast gates, revive survivors or select a source based on published outcomes.
